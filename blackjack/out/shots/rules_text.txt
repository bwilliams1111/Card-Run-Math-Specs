HOW TO PLAY

One bet plays four blackjack hands against one dealer. Every card is drawn from a full deck, so each card is independent of the cards already dealt. The dealer shows one card and draws after you, standing on all 17s.

Your hands play left to right. You choose HIT or STAND on hard 12 to 16 and on soft 17; lower totals are hit for you and higher totals stand. A two-card 21 is a blackjack. Any tie with the dealer counts as a win, except a blackjack against a dealer blackjack, which is a tie (the BJ column still pays it). A dealer blackjack beats every other hand.

The top round is four blackjacks and four wins: 20×, about 1 in 209,000 rounds.

RUN BAR
20× is the build target and the top of the build: four blackjacks and four wins pay exactly 20×. On a 1,000× run you can keep doubling until your total reaches 1,000× your first win, and the double that crosses 1,000× pays in full. No round pays more than the max win of 20,000× your bet.

After any win, including a return of your bet or less, the run bar opens:

CASH OUT takes everything you hold.

DOUBLE OR NOTHING is one hand against the dealer: beat the dealer and the amount doubles. You choose HIT or STAND on hard 12 to 16 and soft 17 and 18; the dealer hits 16 or less and stands on all 17s. You win ties on 19 and 20; any other tie deals the hand again.

DOUBLE HALF doubles half of what you hold and puts the other half (plus any odd cent) in your RUN BANK.

The RUN BANK is paid with everything else when the round ends; if a double loses, the RUN BANK comes back to cash out or double again.

The minimum double is $0.02. A double that pays nothing ends the round.

Best play uses every card on the table — all four hands and the dealer's card — to choose each hit or stand.

Basic play: always hit hard 12; hit hard 13 against 2 and 3; stand on hard 13–16 against 4–6 and on 14–16 against 2 and 3; hit 13–16 against 7 or higher; hit soft 17 except against 7.

LIMITS

Bets: $1.00 to $100.00 in $0.10 steps.

Max win: 20,000× your bet per round. Your operator may set a lower max win; every payout stops at the max win.

Pays are rounded down to the cent.

Results are decided by the game's random number generator. A malfunction voids all pays and plays.

Return-to-player figures are long-run averages for the play described; any single round can return more or less.
